**1. Video Identifier**
*   ICT 2024 Mentorship, Lecture #1, August 5, 2024 [Title].
*   The session topic is the "Foundations of determining a bias".

**2. Core Concept(s) Taught**
*   The market is driven by an algorithm that seeks "smooth" areas (relative equal highs or lows) to disrupt resting orders at specific times of the day.
*   By contrasting "jagged" price action (where liquidity has already been cleared) with "smooth" price action (untested levels), a trader can identify the "draw on liquidity," which is the direction the market is most likely to move.

**3. New Terminology**
*   **Relative Equal Highs/Lows:** Two or more swing highs or lows in close proximity that create a "smooth" level on a chart.
    *   *Plain-language:* A flat price level that looks like resistance or support.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* Double tops or double bottoms.
*   **Priming:** The act of creating or manipulating price to inspire a large number of traders to place their stop-loss orders at a specific level.
    *   *Plain-language:* Setting a trap for other people's stop losses.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* Building liquidity.
*   **Smooth vs. Jagged Price Action:** Smooth areas are untested levels where orders are resting; jagged areas are zones where the market has already "hunted" and cleared orders.
    *   *Plain-language:* Clean levels vs. messy/already-hit levels.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* Fresh vs. tested supply/demand zones.
*   **Pre-market Range:** The 30-minute interval before a session opens, specifically 7:00–7:30 AM, 8:00–8:30 AM, or 9:00–9:30 AM.
    *   *Plain-language:* The setup identification window.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* No clear classical equivalent.
*   **Opening Range:** Specifically defined in this model as the 30-minute window *after* a session start (e.g., 7:30–8:00 AM or 9:30–10:00 AM).
    *   *Plain-language:* The initial execution window.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* Opening Range (though typically 15 minutes in classical TA).
*   **SIBI / BISI:** SIBI stands for Sell-side Imbalance Buy-side Inefficiency (a down-close fair value gap); BISI stands for Buy-side Imbalance Sell-side Inefficiency (an up-close fair value gap).
    *   *Plain-language:* Price gaps or imbalances.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* Gaps.
*   **Volume Imbalance:** A small separation or "gap" between the bodies of candles rather than the wicks.
    *   *Plain-language:* A tiny price gap.
    *   *Classical Equivalent:* No clear classical equivalent.

**4. Mechanical Rules / Setup Criteria**
*   **Time Selection:** Only look for setups at specific New York local times: 7:00–7:30 AM, 8:00–8:30 AM, or 9:00–9:30 AM.
*   **Timeframe Alignment:** Use only the 15-minute, 5-minute, and 1-minute charts.
*   **Identifying Smooth Highs:** If you have two swing highs and the one on the left is slightly higher than the one on the right, it is a high-probability "smooth" target for the algorithm.
*   **Identifying Smooth Lows:** If you have two swing lows and the one on the left is slightly lower than the one on the right, it is a high-probability "smooth" target.
*   **Determining Bias:** 
    *   **If** price action is jagged on the bottom and smooth on the top, **then** the draw on liquidity is the smooth high. 
    *   **If** both relative equal highs and relative equal lows exist, **then** wait for one to be disrupted first and trade toward the remaining smooth side.
*   **Entry Confirmation:** 
    *   **If** price aggressively moves against the previous direction (displacement), **then** look for an entry in the resulting "order block" (the last candle before the move) or a fair value gap.
*   **Invalidation:** If manual intervention (like a major news event or "bombs dropping") occurs, the technical model is temporarily invalidated.
